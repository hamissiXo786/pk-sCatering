What we do

Event formats

Whatever the occasion, we shape the menu, the service and the presentation around you. Here is how most of our clients start.

Hot, generous buffets served from chafing dishes — slow-cooked curries, biryani and rice, roasted vegetables, pasta bakes and fresh salads. Ideal for family functions, office lunches and big celebrations.

A dining experience designed around your day: a canapé reception, a plated first course and a generous main course, styled to suit your décor and your guests.

Ready-made seasonal menus and full-service corporate catering. Our Christmas Lunch Menu 2026 is available now — ask about minimum orders and collection dates.

Individually plated courses with careful presentation — seafood, slow-cooked meats and elegant garnishes, served to the table.

Tacos, sliders and grazing bites set up on rustic tables at farms, gardens and venues — relaxed, shareable and full of flavour.
